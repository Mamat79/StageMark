# StageMark 2027.1.2

After the full 30-day trial, the startup support reminder now counts down for 5 seconds instead of 60. Once the delay ends, StageMark remains fully usable without purchasing. The delay applies only once per process start. A valid license immediately removes the reminder and wait.

The bundled French and English manuals and current explanations are updated. Existing licenses and activations remain compatible; drawing, library, AR, LIVE, calibration, projection and BLACKOUT retain the 2027.1.1 r3 fixes. Electron stays pinned to 43.7.7.

Android companion: build 18 unchanged. macOS delivery uses Codemagic; Apple Silicon qualification and Intel architecture checks are recorded in the delivery receipt. DMGs remain unsigned and unnotarized.

# StageMark 2027.1.7 — updated interface

**Cette livraison concerne Windows 2027.1.7. Les paquets et notices macOS restent en 2027.1.6.** Les nouveaux paquets Mac seront publiés après leur propre qualification. Les anciennes releases sont conservées.

**This release delivers Windows 2027.1.7. macOS packages and specific manuals remain at 2027.1.6.** New Mac packages will be published after their separate qualification. Previous releases are preserved.

The Drawing workspace places plans and layers on the left, properties on the
right and a horizontally scrollable palette below the metric canvas. Fold
either side to enlarge the drawing. Panels can still float; their height is
bounded by the workspace.

SHOW, BLACKOUT and CLOSE OUTPUT remain visible in a fixed strip at the bottom
of the window, including other workspaces. SHOW remains unavailable until an
output is open. AR visibility retains its independence from video blackout.

Dark and light themes are harmonized, the project name stays in the header,
and the inspector presents position and dimensions first. All six alignments,
six distributions, two spacing commands and the Stage/Selection reference are
retained. French and English manuals are updated.

Windows qualification: actual locally built Setup extracted and installed after verified backups; compared personal files and the separate Beta installation were preserved. Detailed checks and limitations are provided separately. macOS qualification remains separate and no new Mac package is claimed by this preparation.
